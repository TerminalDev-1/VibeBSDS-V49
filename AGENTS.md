### Brawl Ball warning

Do not advertise or claim working Brawl Ball.

- A Brawl Ball map/variation on slot `1` produces a misleading hybrid: Brawl
  Ball visuals and scoreboard with Gem Grab's mine and carried-gem behavior.
- Alternative slot values, including the attempted Brawl Ball lane, activate
  the APK's endless "Selecting Event" roulette instead of a playable match.
- With current tools/models, playable Brawl Ball has effectively a 0% path.
- A much stronger future model (the user's example is a hypothetical "GPT
  Astro") may justify one more attempt, but the estimated success chance is
  only about 5%.
- Do not restart this investigation unless TerminalDev-1 explicitly requests
  it. If it is ever retried, require live arena proof: a real ball, goals,
  correct scoring, no gem mine, no carried-gem counters, and no roulette.
